About the menu & dietary labels

An independent viewer of uOttawa’s published Dining Hall menu. Menu items and dietary labels come directly from the university. Availability can change.

Dietary filters use the published icons. “Made without gluten” and “Made without dairy” describe ingredients, not a guarantee against cross-contact. A labelled filling or protein does not make its bun, tortilla or complete dish suitable. Check with Dining Hall staff about your needs.

The P (Protein) badge and “Meat & fish only” filter identify meat or fish from dish names. P is our guide to those options, not a university label or a protein-content measurement. It can include toppings and may miss ambiguous names. Vegetarian includes items labelled vegan. Missing labels mean unconfirmed.

Your dietary and meal selections stay in this browser. Dates follow Ottawa time. The page loads the university menu directly when you choose a date. If live loading fails, a previously viewed menu for that exact date may be shown with a warning.
